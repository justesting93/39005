Events

A duty has a code, a start, an end, and the number of members required. Hours are the time between start and end. An assigned member who is late or leaves early can have fewer attend hours than that.

Training

Training hours count toward the 60-hour year and toward the rule of attending at least 30% of the training hours offered. Tick the members who attended. Attend hours start as the session length, and can be lower when a member is late or leaves early.

Assign

A member is eligible only after the applied checkbox is ticked, and only if they are free of an overlapping duty.

  1. Standard walks the rolling list only. Duty parties take turns, then members take turns inside the party.
  2. Assign gives the duty to the party on turn first. Inside that party it prefers members who have not met this financial year's hours, fewest attend hours first, then fewer duties so far this year, then the member queue. The next party is used only when the party on turn cannot fill the places.
How the sample roster behaves

Twelve members: DP1 is P1–P4, DP2 is P5–P8, DP3 is P9–P12. Everyone has applied for E1–E4. P3 missed induction. P1 also attended the 60-hour camp, so P1 has already met the annual requirement.

Standard, in date order, selects E1: P1, P2 · E2: P5 · E3: P9, P10, P11 · E4: P3.

Assign on E1, before those results are saved, selects P3 then P2. P3 has 0 attend hours. P1 is left off because the requirement is already met.
